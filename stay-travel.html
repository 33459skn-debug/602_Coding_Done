Where to stay

Pick your base

Most visitors base themselves near Khao Kho or Phu Thap Boek for easy access to the sunrise viewpoints, or stay in town for more services and a shorter drive to restaurants.

HIGHLAND RESORTS

Resorts & guesthouses

Small resorts and guesthouses built along the ridges near Khao Kho and Phu Thap Boek, many with a view straight from the room.

CAMPING

Camp on the ridge

Designated camping areas near the sunrise viewpoints let you wake up already in position for the sea of fog.

FARM STAYS

Homestays & farm stays

Family-run stays attached to strawberry and produce farms, a good fit if food and farms are the focus of your trip.

IN TOWN

Guesthouses in town

More options and shorter drives to restaurants and markets, at the cost of a longer trip up to the viewpoints.

How to get there

From Bangkok

BY CAR

Self-drive

The most flexible option and the best for reaching sunrise viewpoints before dawn. Roughly 300–350 km from Bangkok, mostly highway with a mountain climb on the final stretch.

BY BUS

Long-distance bus

Regular coaches run from Bangkok's northern bus terminal to Phetchabun town. Cheapest option, but you'll need a local ride onward to the viewpoints.

BY VAN

Minivan

Faster than the bus and still affordable, with more frequent departures — a common choice for smaller groups without a car.

Getting around Phetchabun

Plan on having your own wheels

Public transport between the viewpoints and farms is limited, so most visitors rent a car or motorbike once they arrive, or arrange a driver for the day. Songthaews run between the larger towns but don't reach most of the mountain viewpoints directly.

Weather & packing tips

What to bring

  • COOL SEASON (NOV–FEB) Warm layers for early mornings — ridgeline temperatures can drop close to jacket weather well before sunrise.
  • RAINY SEASON (JUN–OCT) Waterproof layer and grippy shoes — mountain roads and trails get slick.
  • HOT SEASON (MAR–MAY) Sun protection for daytime, though evenings at altitude still cool off.
  • ALWAYS A flashlight or phone torch for pre-dawn viewpoint hikes, and a reusable water bottle.
