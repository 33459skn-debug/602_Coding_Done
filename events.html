Four ways to spend your time here

Nature & Trekking

Ridge trails and viewpoint roads through Khao Kho and Phu Thap Boek, best walked at dawn before the mist burns off.

Culture & Temples

Local temples and monuments tucked into the highland towns, easy to combine with a viewpoint stop.

Food & Farms

Strawberry picking, farm stalls and highland produce — the cool climate that makes the views also makes the harvest.

Photography Spots

Sea-of-fog sunrises, terraced fields and layered ridgelines — the province's most photographed hours are just after dawn.

Annual calendar

Seasonal highlights

  • NOV – FEB

    Cool season — the best window for the sea of mist, clear ridgeline views and comfortable trekking weather.

  • DEC – FEB

    Strawberry harvest across the highland farms, with picking and roadside stalls at their busiest.

  • DEC – JAN

    Peak sea-of-fog mornings at Phu Thap Boek, when temperature swings between night and day are largest.

  • FEB – MAR

    Cool-season flowers come into bloom across the highlands as the season turns toward the hotter months.

  • YEAR-ROUND

    Local temple fairs and community festivals happen throughout the year — check locally for current dates before you go.
