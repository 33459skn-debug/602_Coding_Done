What to see in the highlands

Natural viewpoints, sunrise seas of fog, and the farms and markets that come with cool mountain air.

Natural attractions

Two mountains, two very
different mornings

Khao Kho

National park Viewpoints Monuments

Khao Kho National Park strings a road of ridgeline viewpoints and monuments across the highest ground in the province. Morning cloud pours through the forest below while the road stays clear above it — pull off at any of the marked lookouts for the view.

Phu Thap Boek

Sunrise point Sea of fog

The province's best-known sunrise spot. Arrive while it's still dark and claim a place on the ridge — as the sky lightens, the entire valley below disappears under a slow, rolling sea of cloud until the sun burns it back.

Food & local products

Cool-climate strawberries

The same cool air that makes the sunrise views possible also grows strawberries most of Thailand can't. Terraced farms across the highlands open their rows for picking through the winter, alongside honey, coffee and other highland produce sold roadside.

Restaurants & markets

A few categories worth building into a trip — ask locally for the current best spot in each, as small stalls and seasonal markets move around.

VIEWPOINT CAFÉS

Coffee with a view

Small cafés built right onto the ridgelines near Khao Kho and Phu Thap Boek, best caught at sunrise.

ROADSIDE FARM STALLS

Strawberries & produce

Fresh-picked strawberries, jams and highland produce sold directly from the farms along the mountain roads.

MORNING MARKETS

Local town markets

Early markets in the district towns for northern Thai breakfast dishes, sticky rice and grilled snacks.

MOUNTAIN KITCHENS

Northern Thai cuisine

Family-run restaurants serving regional dishes suited to the cool climate — hearty stews and grilled pork are local favorites.
